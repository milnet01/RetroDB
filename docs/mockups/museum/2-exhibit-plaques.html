Sega Dreamcast

Sega, 1998 (JP), 1999 (NA, EU)

Sega’s last home console, and the first with a modem built in. It led the sixth generation by more than a year, but sales could not hold off the PlayStation 2, and Sega left the hardware business in 2001.

History written by AI on 2026-09-14.

Before the Dreamcast

The Saturn had sold poorly outside Japan, and Sega needed a clean start. Two teams, one in Japan and one in the United States, competed to design the next machine.

Launch

The Dreamcast launched in Japan in November 1998 and in North America on 9 September 1999. Its American launch broke sales records, helped by Soulcalibur and Sonic Adventure.

Legacy

Online play, a memory card with its own screen, and games such as Shenmue and Jet Set Radio made it a favourite long after it left the shops. Fans kept releasing new games for it for years.
