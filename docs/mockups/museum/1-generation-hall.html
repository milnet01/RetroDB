    Written by AI on 2026-09-14. You can rewrite it at any time.

    Sony entered the games business almost by accident. A deal to build a CD-ROM add-on for the Super Nintendo fell apart in 1991, and engineer Ken Kutaragi persuaded Sony to build its own console instead.

    The PlayStation launched in Japan in December 1994. CDs were cheap to press and held far more than cartridges, which drew publishers away from Nintendo. Games such as Final Fantasy VII, Metal Gear Solid and Gran Turismo made it the machine to own.

    It was the first home console to pass 100 million units, and its success set up Sony for the PlayStation 2.
