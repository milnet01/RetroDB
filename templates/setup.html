What is RetroDB?

RetroDB is a web-based retro gaming ROM library manager. It helps you organize your ROM collection, scrape metadata from multiple sources, track achievements, and more.

  • Organize ROMs across 270+ gaming platforms
  • Scrape metadata from TheGamesDB, IGDB, RAWG, ScreenScraper
  • Track RetroAchievements progress and PS3 trophies
  • ROM tools: archive scanner, CHD converter, duplicate finder
  • Analytics dashboard with collection statistics
